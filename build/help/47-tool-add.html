Add findings to people

This tool adds diseases or phenotypes, or a vital status, to the proband, to everyone or to one person in a GA4GH or Phenopackets file. Diseases and phenotypes can have an age of onset, or be marked as looked for and not found. The drawing has no place for most of these, but the files do.

The form

To
Enter proband, all, affected or unaffected (as the Family's pedigree says), or one person's id, such as IV-4. You can only give findings to people who have a Phenopacket in the file.
Phenotypes, Diseases
Write one term per line, giving its id, then an equals sign and its label, for example HP:0008527=Congenital sensorineural hearing impairment or MONDO:0007254=breast cancer. You can find the terms with Match conditions to terms, or on the HPO and Mondo websites.
These phenotypes were looked for and not found
This marks the phenotypes as excluded. Phenotypes that were looked for and not found are as useful to a diagnosis as those that were found.
Age at onset
An ISO 8601 age, such as P45Y for 45 years or P6M for six months. It applies to every term you add.
Vital status
Alive, deceased or unknown.

If a term comes from an ontology that the file didn't use before, the ontology is declared in the file's metaData, so the file stays valid. Its release is recorded as unknown.

In the terminal, and more

phenotk add /data/examples/okafor_phenopackets-family.json --target IV-4 --phenotype "HP:0008527=Congenital sensorineural hearing impairment" --onset P0D -o ~/results/okafor-added.json
phenotk view ~/results/okafor-added.json

The command can add more than the form can. It can add a variant (--variant --gene HGNC:1884=CFTR --hgvs … --zygosity heterozygous), with --diagnosis naming the disease it explains. It can also add measurements, a cause of death, a date of birth or an age, and any field by its path (--set, --merge, --append). man phenotk and phenotk add --help list them.

In Python

import phenopacket_toolkit as ptk
from phenopacket_toolkit.ops import add

doc = ptk.read("/data/examples/okafor_phenopackets-family.json")
new = add(doc, "IV-4", phenotypes=[{"id_": "HP:0008527", "label": "Congenital sensorineural hearing impairment", "onset": "P0D"}])
ptk.write(new, "/home/user/results/okafor-added.json")
