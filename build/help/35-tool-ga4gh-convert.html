GA4GH Pedigree ⇄ Phenopackets Family

The two GA4GH files describe the same family in two different ways. This tool turns either one into the other. You might use it, for example, when someone has sent you a file in only one of the forms.

The two forms

GA4GH PedigreePhenopackets Family
Who is in iteveryone, each as a Phenopacketthe proband, the relatives with findings, and everyone in its pedigree
How they are relatedrelationships named with KIN terms: parents, donors, adoption, partners, twins, cousins…a PED-style pedigree: father, mother, sex, affected or not
Standardproposed for the GA4GH Pedigree Standard, still being writtenPhenopackets version 2, approved

When you convert to a Family, you lose the narrative and everything a PED-style pedigree can't say: twins, adoptions, separated couples, and the fact that a parent was a donor rather than a partner. When you convert to a GA4GH Pedigree, the relationships come from the parents in the Family's pedigree. The proband's parents are marked as consanguineous if the Family says so and the pedigree doesn't already show it. The GA4GH Pedigree has the details.

The form

The file
Choose a GA4GH Pedigree or a Phenopackets Family.
Make
The tool chooses the other form for you.
Proband
When you're making a Family, give the id of the person to make the proband. If you don't, the tool uses the GA4GH Pedigree's index patient, or else the first affected person.
Save as
The new file is saved as ~/results/name_phenopackets-family.json or …_ga4gh-pedigree.json.

There are some things that each form can't say:

  • If nothing is recorded for a person in the GA4GH Pedigree, they're in the Family's pedigree, but there's no Phenopacket for them among its relatives. Their status in the Family's pedigree is missing (unknown) rather than unaffected. That's because a GA4GH Pedigree records the conditions people have, not that they were found to have none.
  • Going the other way, people who have no Phenopacket in the Family come into the GA4GH Pedigree with only their sex and parents. The Family's pedigree doesn't say whether they're alive.

So only convert between the two forms when you need to. It's better to keep both of the files that studio2ga4gh writes than to make one from the other.

In the terminal

phenotk convert /data/examples/okafor_ga4gh-pedigree.json --to family -o ~/results/okafor-again_phenopackets-family.json
phenotk convert /data/examples/okafor_phenopackets-family.json --to pedigree -o ~/results/okafor-again_ga4gh-pedigree.json

pedigree-ga4gh convert FILE --to pedigree|family -o OUT does the same.

In Python

import phenopacket_toolkit as ptk
from phenopacket_toolkit.ops import to_kind

doc = ptk.read("/data/examples/okafor_ga4gh-pedigree.json")
new, notes = to_kind(doc, "family", proband_id="IV-3")
ptk.write(new, "/home/user/results/okafor-again_phenopackets-family.json")
for note in notes:
    print("note:", note)
