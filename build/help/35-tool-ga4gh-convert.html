GA4GH Pedigree ⇄ Phenopackets Family

The two GA4GH files carry the same family in two ways. This tool turns either into the other – for a file someone has sent you in only one of the forms, for example.

The two forms

GA4GH PedigreePhenopackets Family
Who is in iteveryone, each a Phenopacketthe proband, the relatives with findings, and everyone in its pedigree
How they are relatedrelationships named with KIN terms: parents, donors, adoption, partners, twins, cousins…a PED-style pedigree: father, mother, sex, affected or not
Standardproposed for the GA4GH Pedigree Standard, still being writtenPhenopackets version 2, approved

Going to a Family loses what a PED-style pedigree cannot say – twins, adoptions, separated couples, and that a parent was a donor rather than a partner – and the narrative. Going to a GA4GH Pedigree, the relationships come from the parents in the Family's pedigree; the proband's parents are marked consanguineous if the Family says so and the pedigree does not show it already. The GA4GH Pedigree has the details.

The form

The file
A GA4GH Pedigree or a Phenopackets Family.
Make
The other form (chosen for you).
Proband
Going to a Family: the person's id to make the proband. Without one, the GA4GH Pedigree's index patient is used, or the first affected person.
Save as
~/results/name_phenopackets-family.json or …_ga4gh-pedigree.json.

Some things each form cannot say:

  • A person in the GA4GH Pedigree with nothing recorded is in the Family's pedigree, but has no Phenopacket among its relatives. Their status in the Family's pedigree is missing (unknown) rather than unaffected, because a GA4GH Pedigree records the conditions people have, not that they were found to have none.
  • From a Family, the people with no Phenopacket in it come into the GA4GH Pedigree with their sex and parents only: the Family's pedigree does not say whether they are alive.

So convert each way only when you need to: keep both of the files studio2ga4gh writes, rather than making one from the other.

In the terminal

phenotk convert /data/examples/okafor_ga4gh-pedigree.json --to family -o ~/results/okafor-again_phenopackets-family.json
phenotk convert /data/examples/okafor_phenopackets-family.json --to pedigree -o ~/results/okafor-again_ga4gh-pedigree.json

pedigree-ga4gh convert FILE --to pedigree|family -o OUT does the same.

In Python

import phenopacket_toolkit as ptk
from phenopacket_toolkit.ops import to_kind

doc = ptk.read("/data/examples/okafor_ga4gh-pedigree.json")
new, notes = to_kind(doc, "family", proband_id="IV-3")
ptk.write(new, "/home/user/results/okafor-again_phenopackets-family.json")
for note in notes:
    print("note:", note)
