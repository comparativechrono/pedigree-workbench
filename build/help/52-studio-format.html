The Pedigree Studio file

A drawing is a JSON file, the same whether it is saved with the Studio tab's Save or with Pedigree Studio's own File menu. It describes the drawing – symbols, lines, positions, colours – rather than the family as data; the converters turn it into data.

What is in it

FieldWhat it holds
personsOne entry per symbol: its id, shape (male, female, unknown, their adopted forms, miscarriage for a pregnancy loss, and the no-offspring and infertile bars), position, the text under it, deceased, proband, the mark inside it (centerText: dot for a carrier, line for presymptomatic, D a donor, S a surrogate, P a pregnancy, or a number of people), and how it is filled: fillMode, fillColor, halves, quarters, the shading pattern.
partnershipsThe lines between partners: the two people, and the type (normal, consanguineous, separated, consanguineous-separated).
childLinksThe lines from a couple to each child, dashed for adoption.
twinLinksTwins: the two children, and identical or nonidentical.
legendLabelsThe colour key: each colour (or pattern) and the condition's name. This is how the tools know which condition a fill means.
labels, numberingMode, …Free text placed on the drawing, how generations are numbered, and settings of the layout.

Personal data in a drawing

The text under the symbols is whatever was typed there – often names, ages and dates of birth – and in a drawing made by another program even the ids may be names (p-nnamdi in the examples). Treat a drawing as you would the family's notes. The GA4GH files and the PED files from studio2ped leave the text out and name people by pedigree numbers, unless you ask studio2ped for the text as ids (Drawing → PED or MPED).
