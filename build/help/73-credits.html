Credits and licences

The Pedigree workbench is made by the Hearn lab. It brings Pedigree Studio and the Python packages together in one page, using the framework of the lab's Bioinformatics workbench. It's built from open-source software and open standards, each under its own licence, and THIRD_PARTY.md lists them with their versions.

Standards and data

  • The Phenopackets schema and phenopacket-tools, by the GA4GH. The workbench's checks follow their rules.
  • The Human Phenotype Ontology and the Mondo Disease Ontology, which you search through EBI's Ontology Lookup Service or in copies you download. No copy of either comes with the workbench.
  • The example families come from the lab's pedigree practical, and have been made into Pedigree Studio drawings. The files made from them were written by the packages themselves.
