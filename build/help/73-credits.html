Credits and licences

The Pedigree workbench is the Hearn lab's: Pedigree Studio and the Python packages, brought together in one page with the framework of the lab's Bioinformatics workbench. It is built from open-source software and open standards, each under its own licence; THIRD_PARTY.md lists them with their versions.

Standards and data

  • The Phenopackets schema and phenopacket-tools, by the GA4GH: the workbench's checks follow their rules.
  • The Human Phenotype Ontology and the Mondo Disease Ontology, searched through EBI's Ontology Lookup Service or in copies you download; no copy of them comes with the workbench.
  • The example families are those of the lab's pedigree practical, made into Pedigree Studio drawings; the files made from them are written by the packages themselves.
