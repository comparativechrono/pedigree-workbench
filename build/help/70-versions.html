Versions, and how to cite

These are the versions that this copy of the workbench runs. When you report work done with it, say which versions you used. The files record them too, in their metaData (for example createdBy: studio2ga4gh 0.4.0).

Python itself comes from Pyodide, which is downloaded from jsDelivr the first time Python starts. To see which version you have, type python --version in the terminal, or click the Python pill in the Tools tab.

The same as on PyPI

Every package here except pedwb, the workbench's own runner, PedGen and PedInterp is the version released on PyPI. So pip install on your own computer gives you exactly the code that runs in the workbench. PedGen and PedInterp are built from GitHub, with changes made for the workbench that will be in their next releases. They have their commands, pedgen and pedinterp, and write GA4GH Pedigrees as well as PED files; PedGen writes a table of genotypes for PedNet, and PedInterp reads children drawn straight under their parents, twins, and pedigrees sketched by hand, and, when asked, who has died, the proband and carriers, and writes what it reads as a Pedigree Studio drawing too. The releases of October 2026 brought these changes:

  • PedNet 2.1.0 reads GA4GH Pedigrees and Phenopackets Families as well as PED files, so a family drawn in the Studio, simulated by PedGen or read from a picture by PedInterp goes to it as it is, conditions and all.
  • PedNet 2.0.0 analyses pedigrees as graphs: their loops, kinship and inbreeding, the pattern of affected people, and, with the graph as a Bayesian network, exact likelihoods for the mode of inheritance and for how well candidate variants co-segregate with a condition (PedNet). It keeps the network metric classifier of the earlier PedNet, by Nick Taylor.
  • pedigree-ga4gh 1.0.0 is new. It's the one implementation of the GA4GH Pedigree and of Phenopackets that studio2ga4gh, ga4gh2studio and the phenopacket toolkit share, so they all read and write the same, corrected form (The GA4GH Pedigree).
  • studio2ga4gh 0.4.1, ga4gh2studio 0.4.1 and studio2ped 0.2.1 read a parent marked G as a gestational carrier, not a genetic parent, as they read S. studio2ga4gh writes twins whose zygosity isn't known as isMultipleBirthSiblingOf, and notes a consultand in the narrative, and ga4gh2studio draws them back. ga4gh2studio draws a surrogate who gave the egg as an egg donor (D), since she's a genetic mother. All three read a symbol divided into thirds, one part for each of three conditions, and ga4gh2studio draws three conditions that way. studio2ped and studio2ga4gh number only people, not the bars for no children and infertility, as Pedigree Studio 2.3.0 does.
  • studio2ga4gh, ga4gh2studio and phenopacket-toolkit 0.4.0 read and write their GA4GH files through pedigree-ga4gh. The toolkit also fixes faults in 0.3.0 that made some of its files invalid. Its normalise now fills in a term only when the name is the term's label or one of its synonyms, unless you ask it to accept close matches too.
  • studio2ped 0.2.0 names people by their pedigree numbers by default, as studio2ga4gh does, and puts only genetic parents in the father and mother columns. It also leaves out the no children and infertility bars, which aren't people.
  • ped2studio 0.2.2 keeps apart families that reuse the same ids.
  • hponorm and mondonorm 1.1.0 score the results from EBI's Ontology Lookup Service by how well they match, take synonyms into account, and ignore capital letters in terms files. They no longer treat names that are spelt alike but mean different things, such as hypothyroidism and hyperthyroidism, as close matches, and they search a local copy of an ontology much faster.

The releases before these were 0.3.0 of studio2ga4gh, ga4gh2studio and phenopacket-toolkit, 0.1.0 of studio2ped, 0.2.1 of ped2studio, and 1.0.0 of hponorm and mondonorm. mpedtools is still at version 0.1.0. On your own computer explains how to install the packages outside the browser.
