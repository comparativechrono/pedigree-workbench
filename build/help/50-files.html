Your files

Your drawings, your notebooks and the files the tools write are all kept in a home folder, ~ (/home/user), which every tab shares. The Files tab shows them, along with the examples.

The folders

FolderWhat goes in it
~/pedigreesdrawings (.studio.json) saved with the Studio's Save as…, and drawings the tools make
~/resultswhat the tools write, unless you choose another folder
~/termsterms files, from Match conditions to terms
~/ontologiescopies of the HPO and Mondo, for searching without the internet
~/notebooksnotebooks, starting with pedigree-tools.ipynb
~/datafiles added from your computer with a tool's From my computer…
~/examplesyour own copies of the examples, made with Copy to my files
/data/examplesthe examples, which can't be changed. To change one, copy it with Copy to my files.

The Files tab

Click a file to look at it. JSON, PED files and text open in an editor, where you save your changes with Save or Ctrl+S. A drawing opens in the Studio tab, and a notebook in the Notebook tab. The buttons above a pedigree file lead to the tools that use it: Draw in Studio, Check, Convert… and More…. Each file and folder also has a menu (⋯), where you can rename, duplicate, delete or download it, copy its path, or open its folder in the terminal.

Adding files

  • My files → Add files from my computer… or Add a folder…, at the top right. You can also drag files onto the Files tab.
  • In a folder's menu: Upload files here….
  • In a tool, under a file box: From my computer….
  • In the Studio: Open… → Open a drawing from my computer….

Downloading files

  • Download, next to a file a tool has written, or in a file's menu.
  • Download as a .zip, in a folder's menu.
  • My files → Download all my files (.zip), for everything in your home folder.
  • In the terminal: download FILE, or zip then download.

Where the files are kept

Your files stay in this browser, on this computer. The workbench keeps them in the browser's own storage for this site (IndexedDB), so they're still there when you come back. They aren't sent anywhere, and they aren't on any other computer, or in another browser on this one.

  • The browser may not keep them in a private window, and it deletes them if you clear this site's data. Saved in this browser, at the top right, tells you whether they're being kept.
  • Browsers give each site a share of the disk, which is usually plenty. My files shows how much is used.
  • On a computer that other people use, anyone who opens the workbench in your browser account can see your files. My files → Start afresh deletes them all, along with your notebooks and the drawing in the Studio.
Keep copies of what matters

A browser's storage isn't a backup. Download anything you need to keep, especially your drawings, and store it with the rest of your work.
