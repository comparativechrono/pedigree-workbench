KIN, the kinship terms

The Kinship Ontology, KIN, is the GA4GH Pedigree Standard's vocabulary of relationships. Each relationship in a GA4GH Pedigree is one KIN term, such as KIN:027 isBiologicalMotherOf.

How a relationship is written

{"individualId": "III-3", "relation": {"id": "KIN:027", "label": "isBiologicalMotherOf"}, "relativeId": "IV-3"}

It reads as a sentence: III-3 is the biological mother of IV-3. The label is taken word for word from KIN. A symmetric relation, such as one between partners, twins or cousins, is written only once, in one direction.

Brothers and sisters, grandparents, aunts and cousins aren't written, because they follow from the parents.

The 48 relationship terms

The terms come from kin.owl in KIN's repository, ga4gh/pedigree_family_history_terminology (commit 71616ea of 16 September 2024, which is still its latest). This is the file behind http://purl.org/ga4gh/kin.owl. KIN also has KIN:031, hasSex, but that's a property of one person rather than a relationship between two people. So the table leaves it out, and the workbench never uses it.

Retired codes

Earlier drafts of KIN had codes that the current version doesn't have, and some of them turn up in older files. The check reports them, and the upgrade replaces them:

KIN version 1, in draft

A revised KIN is being drafted alongside version 1 of the standard (see The GA4GH Pedigree), and it hasn't been merged yet. It would add 22 terms (KIN:200 to KIN:221), which tell biological relatives apart from social and legal ones. It would also retire ten terms. Among them are KIN:003, which KIN:200 would replace, and KIN:030 and KIN:051, whose consanguinity would become a flag on the relationship.

Ten codes would keep their number but have a narrower meaning. For example, KIN:017 isGrandparentOf would become isSocialLegalGrandparentOf. Until the draft is merged, the terms above are KIN's current ones, and they're what the workbench writes.
