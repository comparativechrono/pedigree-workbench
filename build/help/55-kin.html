KIN, the kinship terms

The Kinship Ontology, KIN, is the GA4GH Pedigree Standard's vocabulary of relationships: each relationship in a GA4GH Pedigree is one KIN term, such as KIN:027 isBiologicalMotherOf.

How a relationship is written

{"individualId": "III-3", "relation": {"id": "KIN:027", "label": "isBiologicalMotherOf"}, "relativeId": "IV-3"}

It reads as a sentence: III-3 is the biological mother of IV-3. The label is KIN's own, as it is written in KIN. A symmetric relation (partners, twins, cousins) is written once, in one direction.

Brothers and sisters, grandparents, aunts and cousins are not written: they follow from the parents.

Retired codes

Earlier drafts of KIN had codes that the current one does not; some turn up in older files. The check reports them, and the upgrade replaces them:

KIN version 1, in draft

A revised KIN is being drafted with version 1 of the standard (see The GA4GH Pedigree), and is not yet merged. It would add 22 terms (KIN:200 to KIN:221), which tell biological relatives apart from social and legal ones, and retire ten, among them KIN:003 (replaced by KIN:200) and KIN:030 and KIN:051, whose consanguinity would become a flag on the relationship. Ten codes would keep their number with a narrower meaning: KIN:017 isGrandparentOf, for instance, would become isSocialLegalGrandparentOf. Until the draft is merged, the terms above are KIN's current ones, and they are what the workbench writes.
