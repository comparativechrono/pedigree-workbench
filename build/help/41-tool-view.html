Look inside a GA4GH file

Shows a GA4GH Pedigree, a Phenopackets Family, Cohort or Phenopacket without its JSON: a table of the people and their terms, or the numbers of people, terms and relationships. The quickest way to see that a file says what you meant.

What it shows

A table of the people and their terms
A line for each person: their id (the proband marked *), sex, affected status, deceased if they have died, and their terms. For a GA4GH Pedigree, the relationships follow, in words (III-2 isBiologicalFatherOf IV-3). For a Family, the people of its pedigree who have no Phenopacket are listed at the end.
Counts
The number of people, by sex and by affected status; of phenotypic features (and how many are excluded) and diseases; of terms that still have no id; and the commonest terms.

In a GA4GH Pedigree the affected status is MISSING for everyone without a term, because the file records the conditions people have, not that anyone was found to have none.

In the terminal

phenotk view /data/examples/okafor_ga4gh-pedigree.json
phenotk stats /data/examples/okafor_phenopackets-family.json

In Python

import phenopacket_toolkit as ptk
from phenopacket_toolkit.ops import render, stats, render_stats

doc = ptk.read("/data/examples/okafor_phenopackets-family.json")
print(render(doc))
print(render_stats(stats(doc)))
