Look inside a GA4GH file

This tool shows you a GA4GH Pedigree, or a Phenopackets Family, Cohort or Phenopacket, without its JSON. You can see a table of the people and their terms, or the numbers of people, terms and relationships. It's the quickest way to see that a file says what you meant.

What it shows

A table of the people and their terms
There's a line for each person, with * marking the proband. Each line gives the person's id, sex, affected status, deceased if they have died, and their terms. For a GA4GH Pedigree, the relationships follow in words, such as III-2 isBiologicalFatherOf IV-3. For a Family, the people in its pedigree who have no Phenopacket are listed at the end.
Counts
You'll see the number of people, by sex and by affected status, and the numbers of phenotypic features and diseases, with how many of the features are excluded. You'll also see how many terms still have no id, and which terms are the commonest. For a GA4GH Pedigree, the relationships are counted too, kind by kind.

In a GA4GH Pedigree, the affected status is MISSING for everyone without a term. This is because the file records the conditions people have, not that anyone was found to have none.

In the terminal

phenotk view /data/examples/okafor_ga4gh-pedigree.json
phenotk stats /data/examples/okafor_phenopackets-family.json

In Python

import phenopacket_toolkit as ptk
from phenopacket_toolkit.ops import render, stats, render_stats

doc = ptk.read("/data/examples/okafor_phenopackets-family.json")
print(render(doc))
print(render_stats(stats(doc)))
