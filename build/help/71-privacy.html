Privacy

The workbench is a web page that does its work in your browser. Your drawings and files aren't uploaded anywhere, and there's no server behind the page that sees them.

What stays on this computer

  • Your files, drawings and notebooks, which are kept in this browser's storage for the site (Your files).
  • The drawing that's open in the Studio tab, which Pedigree Studio keeps in the same storage.
  • Small settings: the tab you were on, the help page you were reading, and the name you gave the terminal.

The page has no cookies, no accounts and no analytics.

Patient data

Pedigrees are personal data about whole families, including people who haven't consented to anything. Use the workbench as your institution's rules on patient data allow, and follow this advice:

  • Prefer pedigree numbers to names. The GA4GH and PED files use them by default, and the drawing is the only place names need to be.
  • If you name people in any other way, such as by the drawing's labels, Pedigree Studio's ids or the text under the symbols, check the ids before you share the file, because they're often names (Drawing → PED or MPED).
  • De-identify files before you pass them on, and keep the key separately (De-identify). Remember that de-identified doesn't mean anonymous.
  • On a shared computer, download your work and use My files → Start afresh when you finish.
