PED or MPED → drawing

ped2studio draws a PED or MPED file in Pedigree Studio: the people, their parents and partners, and who is affected.

The form

The PED file
A .ped, .fam or .mped file.
Save the drawing as
A .studio.json file, in ~/pedigrees unless you choose otherwise.
Colour for affected people, Fill, Shade with a pattern
How the affected people (status 2) are marked: a colour, over the whole symbol or its left half, or stripes or dots instead of a fill. An MPED file's conditions each get a colour of their own.
Lay it out afresh with Pedigree Studio's Tidy when it opens
Done in the Studio when you open the drawing from the result.

What comes out

Founders, couples and their children, generation by generation; the condition in the key, named Affected (or, from an MPED file, after each column); each person labelled with their id. A PED file says nothing of twins, deaths, adoptions or pregnancies, nor what the condition is: add those in the Studio, and name the condition in the key so that it can be given a term. Several families in one file are drawn side by side. Draw a PED file you have been sent goes through it step by step.

In the terminal

ped2studio /data/examples/lynch.ped ~/pedigrees/lynch.studio.json --colour '#e67e22'
studio --tidy ~/pedigrees/lynch.studio.json
Option
--colour HEX (or --color)the colour for affected people (default #1a2332)
--fill full|halfthe whole symbol, or its left half
--shading, --pattern stripes|dotsa pattern instead of a fill

In Python

from ped2studio import convert_file

session = convert_file("/data/examples/lynch.ped", "/home/user/pedigrees/lynch.studio.json",
                       affected_color="#e67e22", use_shading=False, shading_pattern="stripes", fill_type="full")
print(len(session["persons"]), "people")
